### Original reproduction

`v2-api-c4-before.log`: **2 files / 3 tests failed**, 5.76s, against unchanged source-v2 production files. An accepted non-auto-completing turn materializes completed assistant/tool messages, then a correctly located non-delta notification enters the **actual EventHub callback**. The real-gap case also fails to surface explicit publication uncertainty.

The cause is not native protocol, consent or epoch drift: `hub.reconciled()` returned false simply because its healthy caller was draining. Recovery called `scheduleRepair()`, immediately incrementing runtime dirty generation. The callback's post-read validator then failed; canonical guards correctly discarded now-stale output. Adapter IDs and `terminalDelivered` had already treated enqueue as delivery, suppressing later re-emission. Original S1/S2/C1–C3 reproduction logs and source-v1/v2 artifacts remain unchanged.

### Narrow correction and boundary choice

- A matching **healthy** owner needs neither a replacement delivery signal nor a new generation to reconcile inside its own callback. A genuinely dirty owner still cannot rearm while draining: it requests one fresh caller-owned read through `onDrained`, after unwind. That request conveys no publication permission and does not clear dirty state or reuse a stale snapshot.
- Routine polls/deferred repair queue work without incrementing dirty generation. Genuine `onDirty` and explicit invalidation still increment it; hub generation, signals, consent, epoch and exact-owner validators remain intact. The pending repair flag coalesces work; benign draining does not create a re-entrant retry loop.
- If an **actual downstream guard** drops owned output because of a real gap/invalidation, an internal object-identity rejection observer reports **explicit canonical-publication uncertainty** for that exact current owner/epoch/turn. It clears the local `terminalDelivered` claim and publishes a safe error state under current authority. It does **not** remove emitted IDs, replay previously accepted messages/tools, rewrite native/journal evidence, re-admit prompts or infer a universal PubSub/multi-consumer acknowledgement. This uses the user-authorized uncertainty alternative rather than a broad delivery-ack/protocol rewrite.
- Rejection observers are optional, weakly held, one-shot per record and exception-contained. They grant no new authority, do not transfer permits between owners/threads and do not change serialized schemas. Disposed/superseded owners and different turns are excluded. S1 canonical enqueue/delivery guards and exact-owner cleanup exceptions are not weakened.
